# qq-ui

Holding place until this plugin has a wiki.

Independent regions. Same rule as Cordis fibers: a piece that did not change is not torn down, and does not hitch its neighbors.

This console is HTMX. One `sse-connect`, named events, named `sse-swap` targets. Status is not the transcript. The composer is not the heading. Popups are not the chrome. The drawer and project rail live outside the session stream.

Do not replace `#session-panel` as one blob because something in the session changed. POSTs that really change the whole session may swap a larger target; streaming and status ticks may not.

Stay on this stack. Do not add a second UI framework. Morphing a god-node is not independence.
